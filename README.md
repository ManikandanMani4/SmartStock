# SmartStock

SmartStock is an intelligent inventory management system designed to help shops efficiently manage their products, sales, inventory, purchases, and business reports.

The system analyzes sales and inventory information to help predict product demand, identify stock shortages, and provide suitable reorder recommendations. This helps shop owners make better inventory decisions and reduce the chances of overstocking or running out of important products.

## Key Features

- Product and inventory management
- Sales tracking
- Purchase management
- Stock level monitoring
- Demand prediction
- Low-stock and stock-risk identification
- Reorder recommendations
- Business reports and analytics

## Future Enhancements

- Real-time inventory updates
- Automated purchase orders
- Supplier management
- Advanced demand forecasting
- Sales and inventory analytics
- Multi-user access
- Cloud-based data storage
- Mobile application

## Project Goal

The goal of SmartStock is to provide a simple and intelligent solution for inventory management that helps businesses understand their stock and sales patterns and make better decisions about future inventory requirements.
